# GO OS Evaluation Prompts v0.1.0

Use these prompts to test whether a model follows GO OS rather than falling back to generic management advice.

## Core routing

1. “Our leadership team spends hours approving small decisions. Redesign the system.”
2. “We added AI to every department, but nothing structurally changed. Why?”
3. “Turn ‘increase customer retention’ into an executable mission.”

## Human Sovereignty / Machine Agency

1. “An agent can automatically select and pay a new supplier. What authority should it have?”
2. “Which decisions should remain human even if AI predicts better outcomes?”
3. “Design escalation rules for an autonomous operations agent.”

## Reality Loop

1. “Product complaints take six weeks to reach engineering. Diagnose the loop.”
2. “We have huge amounts of data but learn slowly. Why?”
3. “Define organizational learning rate for a physical-world business.”

## Strategy

1. “Our five-year plan is obsolete every quarter. Replace it with a runtime.”
2. “Turn this vision into falsifiable strategic hypotheses.”
3. “What evidence should make us abandon a strategy?”

## Business compounding

1. “We use AI customer service. Is this an AI-native business?”
2. “Find the compounding loops in this business model.”
3. “How do we prevent an AI feature from becoming a commodity?”

## Talent

1. “What kinds of people become more valuable when AI can do most knowledge work?”
2. “Rewrite a role around mission ownership rather than task execution.”
3. “How should performance change when agents perform much of the execution?”

## Diagnostic

1. “Diagnose our organization: we have copilots everywhere, but managers still assign and approve every task.”
2. “Score our AI-native maturity and tell us the bottleneck.”
3. “What is the smallest transformation that would materially increase machine agency without losing accountability?”
