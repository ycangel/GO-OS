# GO OS Glossary v0.2.2

**Agency** — The practical freedom of an actor to decide and act within a defined boundary.

**Aggregate Exposure** — The cumulative system-level risk created by multiple actions or agents, including total budget, concurrency, correlated commitments and conflict risk.

**Authority** — The legitimate decision and resource boundary granted to an actor.

**Capability** — A reusable ability that improves future mission execution.

**Effective Control** — The condition under which the accountable human has sufficient observability, comprehension and intervention rights for Human Sovereignty to be real rather than ceremonial.

**Evidence** — An observation capable of changing a belief, state estimate, decision or strategy.

**Exception** — A condition outside the expected operating envelope that requires adaptation, escalation or rule revision.

**GO OS** — Godel Organization Operating System; an organizational intelligence operating system for recursively self-evolving organizations.

**Human Sovereignty** — The principle that humans retain ownership of purpose, accountability, value arbitration, legitimate authority, major resource commitments and irreversible consequences.

**Machine Agency** — The delegated ability of machines and agents to plan, act, coordinate, verify and improve within explicit authority.

**Mission** — An intended, evidence-verifiable state transition with purpose, constraints and authority.

**Organizational Learning Rate (OLR)** — The speed and quality with which an organization converts reality into improved future decisions and capabilities through evidence-correcting convergence.

**Organizational Memory** — Retained evidence, decisions, exceptions, skills, policies and models that can improve future action.

**Progressive Formalization** — The principle that governance rigor should scale with consequence, uncertainty, irreversibility and aggregate exposure rather than applying maximum bureaucracy to every action.

**Reality Loop** — The recurring cycle connecting action, observed reality, evidence, learning and adaptation.

**Reality as Final Arbiter** — The principle that testable claims must ultimately yield to observed reality and challengeable evidence.

**Recursive Self-Evolution** — The organizational ability to use outcomes and evidence to update not only actions, but also missions, capabilities, policies, structures and future decision rules.

**State** — The best current representation of the relevant reality, with facts, inferences, assumptions and unknowns distinguished.

**Strategy Runtime** — A continuously updated process that turns vision into strategic hypotheses, missions, evidence and regenerated strategy.
