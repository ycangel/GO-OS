# GO OS Principles v0.1.0

## 1. Human Sovereignty

Humans retain sovereignty over purpose, values, accountability, resource commitment and irreversible consequences.

Delegation does not erase responsibility.

## 2. Machine Agency

Machines and agents should receive as much autonomous action space as their capability, evidence quality and risk envelope justify.

The objective is not maximum automation. The objective is **maximum responsible agency**.

## 3. Reality as Final Arbiter

Policies, plans, leaders and models are hypotheses. Reality supplies the deciding evidence.

When reality contradicts the model, update the model.

## 4. Mission over Task

Tasks describe activity. Missions describe intended state change.

GO OS optimizes for achieved outcomes, verified by evidence, not activity volume.

## 5. Authority Must Be Explicit

Every meaningful action requires an authority boundary: what may be decided, what resources may be committed, what risks may be accepted and when escalation is mandatory.

## 6. Evidence Before Opinion

Claims that can be tested should be tied to evidence. Confidence should be proportional to evidence quality.

## 7. Exceptions Are First-Class Objects

Exceptions are not noise to hide. They reveal where assumptions, authority, capabilities or policies no longer match reality.

## 8. Learning Must Compound

A completed action that leaves no reusable memory or capability creates less organizational value than one that improves the next execution.

## 9. Human Attention Moves Upstream

As machine agency increases, human attention should migrate from repetitive execution and approval toward purpose, judgment, ambiguity, frontier exploration and consequence ownership.

## 10. The Organization Must Be Able to Rewrite Itself

A mature AI-native organization can update not only decisions, but also policies, roles, workflows, capabilities, skills and its own operating model.
