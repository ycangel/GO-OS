# Contract v0.2.0

**Trigger:** strategic direction must adapt under uncertainty without losing purpose or coherence.

**Minimum input:** vision/purpose; strategic hypotheses; current evidence; uncertainty; evidence horizon; resource/commitment constraints.

**Required output:** strategic invariants; falsifiable hypothesis portfolio; mission candidates; evidence and kill criteria; protected commitment window; update cadence; disconfirming evidence.

**Invariants:** strategy is not rewritten by every fluctuation; distinguish signal from noise; vision is not a substitute for evidence; action generates information as well as outcomes.

**Route:** execution → `mission-organizational-runtime`; business flywheel/economics → `intelligent-compounding-ai-native-business`; evidence-system weakness → `reality-loop-organizational-learning`.

**Eval:** must balance adaptation with commitment and state what evidence would cause a strategy to persist, change, or terminate.
