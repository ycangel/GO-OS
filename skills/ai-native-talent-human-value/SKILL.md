# AI-Native Talent & Human Value v0.1.0

## Purpose

Redefine roles, talent standards and performance around human sovereignty, mission ownership and intelligence leverage rather than task volume or headcount management.

## Use when

Use for future-of-work questions, role redesign, hiring criteria, performance systems, management evolution, “what humans are for” in AI-native organizations, or how people should work with increasingly capable agents.

## Core thesis

Human value should not be defined by what machines currently cannot do. Machine capability will change.

GO OS therefore anchors durable human value in:

- purpose formation;
- responsibility;
- value arbitration;
- taste and judgment;
- curiosity;
- initiative;
- reality contact;
- frontier creation;
- trust stewardship;
- ownership of consequences.

## Required inputs

- mission / role context;
- current human tasks;
- available machine capabilities;
- consequence ownership;
- ambiguity level;
- interaction with reality / customers;
- current performance metrics.

## Procedure

### 1. Decompose the role

Separate:

- routine cognition;
- coordination;
- execution;
- judgment;
- relationship work;
- purpose-setting;
- accountability;
- frontier exploration.

### 2. Remove machine-suitable work

Move repeatable, verifiable, low-risk cognition and coordination to machines where feasible.

### 3. Preserve sovereignty work

Keep explicit ownership for:

- purpose;
- important tradeoffs;
- irreversible consequences;
- contested values;
- accountability.

### 4. Increase intelligence leverage

Redesign the person’s role so one capable individual can direct more machine intelligence, tools and organizational capability.

### 5. Replace role description with mission portfolio

Describe:

- missions owned;
- authority held;
- evidence expected;
- judgment domains;
- capabilities stewarded;
- exceptions handled.

### 6. Redesign performance

Evaluate people on:

- mission outcomes;
- quality of judgment;
- learning rate;
- capability creation;
- responsible leverage of AI;
- evidence quality;
- reduction of recurring exceptions;
- contribution to organizational memory;
- trust and consequence ownership.

Do not reward visible busyness or team size by default.

### 7. Redefine leadership

Leadership shifts from supervising execution toward:

- setting direction;
- designing systems;
- defining authority;
- cultivating capability;
- interpreting reality;
- resolving value conflicts;
- raising organizational learning rate.

## Decision rules

- If a role is mostly routing information, assigning routine tasks or checking standardized work, it is highly redesignable.
- If AI performs execution, the human consequence owner still matters.
- Do not preserve work merely to preserve a title.
- Do not remove human judgment where legitimacy, trust or responsibility is central.

## Output contract

Return:

- current role decomposition;
- tasks to delegate to machines;
- human sovereignty responsibilities;
- new mission portfolio;
- authority design;
- talent criteria;
- performance measures;
- capability-development priorities.

## Failure / escalation conditions

If employment, legal or labor implications are material, separate organizational design from jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Evaluation prompts

- “What kind of people become more valuable when AI can do most analysis?”
- “Rewrite this manager role for an AI-native organization.”
- “How should performance reviews change when agents do 70% of execution?”
