# Contract v0.2.0

**Trigger:** decision rights, approvals, autonomy, resource commitments, accountability, reversibility, or escalation is the primary problem.

**Minimum input:** action/domain; accountable human; consequence; reversibility; risks; resource exposure; current authority.

**Required output:** sovereignty boundary; autonomy envelope; schema-compatible `AuthorityGrant`; effective-control requirements; revocation/escalation; supporting and disconfirming evidence.

**Invariants:** sovereignty is not micromanagement; accountability without observability/intervention is invalid; agents cannot expand their own authority; aggregate multi-agent exposure must be bounded.

**Route:** mission execution → `mission-organizational-runtime`; structural authority defect → `ai-native-organization-design`; recurring exceptions → `reality-loop-organizational-learning`.

**Eval:** must reject unlimited authority and must distinguish reversible delegated action from irreversible consequence ownership.
