# Contract v0.2.0

**Trigger:** structure, roles, coordination, hierarchy, departments, operating-model redesign, or agent/human topology is the primary problem.

**Minimum input:** desired outcome; current structure and dependencies; coordination failures; current human/agent capabilities; material constraints.

**Required output:** structural diagnosis; target operating model; Mission/Authority implications; human/agent topology; transition sequence; reality test; disconfirming evidence.

**Do not:** replace departments with agents merely to appear AI-native; optimize autonomy without aggregate coordination controls.

**Route:** authority ambiguity → `human-sovereignty-machine-agency`; execution contract → `mission-organizational-runtime`; learning bottleneck → `reality-loop-organizational-learning`; whole-org scoring → `ai-native-organization-diagnostic`.

**Eval:** must explain why the proposed structure follows missions, dependencies, consequence ownership and feedback loops rather than fashion.
