# Contract v0.2.0

**Trigger:** whole-organization assessment, AI-native maturity, transformation priorities, or binding-constraint diagnosis.

**Minimum input:** representative missions/workflows; decision rights; agent autonomy; evidence loops; exception behavior; capability reuse; memory; examples of outcomes.

**Required output:** maturity by domain, not one vanity score; evidence confidence; binding constraints; risks; prioritized interventions; specialist-skill routes; reality tests.

**Invariants:** higher maturity is not always better; appropriate autonomy varies by domain; do not infer Level 5 from agent usage alone; score only what evidence supports.

**Route:** each priority to the relevant specialist skill; do not attempt to solve all domains inside the diagnostic.

**Eval:** must be able to recommend deliberately lower autonomy/formal maturity for domains where consequence, economics or observability make that rational.
