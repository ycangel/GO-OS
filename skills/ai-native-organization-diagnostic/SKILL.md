# AI-Native Organization Diagnostic v0.2.1

## Purpose
Assess AI-native maturity, identify the dominant constraint, and propose the smallest high-leverage transformation toward responsible machine agency and faster organizational learning.

## Required inputs
Organization purpose; recurring missions; current AI use; decision and approval structure; feedback loops; knowledge and memory; bottlenecks; risk boundaries. If inputs are sparse, score with confidence bands and list missing evidence.

## Diagnostic dimensions
Score 0–5: D1 Mission Native; D2 Machine Agency; D3 Human Sovereignty Clarity; D4 Authority Design; D5 Reality Loop Closure; D6 Evidence Quality; D7 Organizational Memory; D8 Capability Compounding; D9 Strategy Runtime; D10 Self-Revision.

## Maturity bands
0.0–0.9 Tool Augmentation; 1.0–1.9 Workflow Automation; 2.0–2.9 Agentic Execution; 3.0–3.9 Mission Runtime; 4.0–4.5 Organizational Learning; 4.6–5.0 Self-Revision. The mean is descriptive, not definitive.

## Procedure
1. Gather examples and evidence, not slogans.
2. Score dimensions with confidence, evidence and missing evidence.
3. Identify the dominant constraint preventing progress elsewhere.
4. Select a recurring mission with high learning value and bounded downside.
5. Define target changes in Mission, Authority, Evidence, Exception, Capability and Memory.
6. Define 30/60/90-day evidence of changed organizational behavior, not adoption vanity metrics.

## Decision rules
AI usage and agent count are not maturity. Automation without authority redesign usually stalls below Mission Runtime. High autonomy without evidence and sovereignty is unmanaged risk. Do not optimize maturity score for its own sake.

## Output contract
Return maturity level; 10-dimension scorecard with confidence; dominant constraint; mission wedge; target operating model; 30/60/90-day steps; progress evidence; risks and sovereignty gates.
